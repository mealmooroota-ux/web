## [STORY]

This ring was born almost playfully, starting from the form of a realistic beetle that was deliberately deconstructed and transformed into something new. The rigor of the figure was released, giving way to freedom and experimentation, dissolving into a more abstract and spontaneous gesture. A light yet meaningful creative process that speaks of transformation: as in life, what ends does not disappear, but changes form and continues to exist, revealing unexpected beauty. This ring was born almost playfully, starting from the form of a realistic beetle that was deliberately deconstructed and transformed into something new. The rigor of the figure was released, giving way to freedom and experimentation, dissolving into a more abstract and spontaneous gesture.
