GilGa: Smooth slither, vivacious scales, fierce fangs and enigmatic eyes of the mighty snake, GilGa is an extensive collection which unravels the tale of the unyielding loyalty of GilGa’s devotees, who roam among us veiled in secrecy.
Each piece is infused with the serpentine spirit, channeling the strength, grace, and mystique of the sacred serpent. Enigmatic earrings mirror the fluid elegance of a snake’s form, while enchanting rings embody its raw might. Belts, bracelets, and necklaces wrap the wearer with GilGa’s force, while adorned accessories—cufflinks, hairpins, tie tops, and handcuffs—enhance the allure and summon her essence.
The collection is for those whose silent reverence hides a fierce, eternal bond for the hiss-tory…
