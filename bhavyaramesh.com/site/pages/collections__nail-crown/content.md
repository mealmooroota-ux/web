## Every Nail Deserves a Crown

Rule in the BR crowning jewels; power, grace, elegance, and control (basically, the world at your fingertips). One-of-a-kind, unique product category, nail rings are a Bhavya Ramesh Jewelry’s speciality and a pioneering concept. Handcrafted, detailed sterling silver jewelry for your nails, made with precision. Nail art and nail design reimagined as a bolder expression of self. Even more fuss-free than clip-on or stick-on nails. No polish, no glue. These rings come in an adjustable fit for all glorious hands, transforming them into a statement of their own, depending on the vibe and the aura.

Each piece in this collection is a celebration of individuality and made to empower, there is a crown for every type of queen. Whether you are subtle and poised, feminine and elegant, fierce and intimidating, a princess out of a fairytale, or a queen from the chapters of history - the Nail Crown has you covered. These metallic nails are more than just adornments or embellishments, they’re an assertion.

You can wear them solo or personalise the look further by mixing and matching to create your own unique set of claws. Jewelry like this turns heads no matter the outfit or occasion; everyday casuals, night-out looks, costume party fits, or festive wear.

You may ask what is the significance of a nail ring? We believe style is personal and stylish is unique. It should be free-spirited, experimental, and most importantly, unapologetically you. Curating pieces that resonate with you, with attention to the smallest details, is what makes your closet truly yours…

Grace in every gesture. Crown in every detail.

## Why Choose Bhavya Ramesh Nail Rings?

- Innovative Jewelry Category: One-of-a-kind concept introducing wearable nail jewelry, a Bhavya Ramesh signature.
- Sterling Silver Craftsmanship: Each nail ring is hand-sculpted in 92.5 sterling silver, finished with care by skilled artisans, it’s not only a bold, contemporary piece of jewelry but also a good investment.
- Versatile & Fuss-Free: No glue, no polish. Just slip it on. Adjustable for all nail sizes and finger shapes.
- Design Language: From minimalist French-tip silhouettes to regal pearl-dripped tassels, every piece tells a story.
- Culturally Rooted, Boldly Expressive: A blend of traditional Indian craftsmanship with experimental fashion identity.

## FAQs – Nail Crown Collection

What is the purpose of nail rings? Nail rings are a unique jewelry category. Unlike fake nails or stick-ons, it doesn’t require glue or polish and is made from durable metals like sterling silver. It is a luxurious, more elated version of nail art/ nail design, it sits over your fingernail, like a crown.

Can you get adjustable rings? Yes. All nail rings by Bhavya Ramesh come with adjustable sizing to comfortably fit different fingers and nail lengths. All Nails deserve their own Crown.

How long do clip-on nails last? As long as you want them to. Sturdy, adjustable, durable, meant to be a timeless piece of statement jewelry by Bhavya Ramesh. Our designs ensure a snug fit, which can be easily clipped on while staying comfortable, even with hand movement. They’re designed to be worn confidently.

How do you wear a nail ring? Wear it as a standalone statement or stack multiple designs. It complements both traditional and contemporary outfits, a perfect conversation started and a guaranteed head-tuner. There are no rules when it comes to styling the BR Jewels.

Are nail sets reusable? Yes! Whether it is individual or worn as a full stack/ set, the Bhavya Ramesh Nail Rings are made in Sterling Silver. Each piece is designed to last and can be worn repeatedly, it’s jewelry, not disposable nail art.
